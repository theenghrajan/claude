When to install

When Asphalt Roof Installation May Be Appropriate

Asphalt roof installation is intended for projects requiring a completely new roofing system rather than work on an existing roof. This may include a newly built home receiving its first roof, a new residential structure that requires complete roofing, or another project where composition roofing has been selected as the preferred roofing system.

Every installation is planned around the property’s design and the goals established before construction is complete.

Although asphalt roofing is a suitable choice for many residential projects, it is not automatically the right solution for every property. The appropriate roofing system depends on the structure, project requirements, and conditions the roof will experience throughout the year.

Installation vs. replacement

How New Asphalt Installation Differs From Replacement

Asphalt roof installation generally applies to new homes, new structures, or projects requiring a completely new roofing system. Asphalt roof replacement, on the other hand, involves removing and replacing an existing asphalt roof after evaluating its condition and determining that replacement is the appropriate solution.
